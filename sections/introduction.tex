\chapter{Introduction}

This document provides an overview of the requirements for preparing a master thesis at the Chair of Software Engineering at the University of Mannheim. It also serves as a template for the format of the thesis, which is available in both a LaTeX\footnote{An introduction to using LaTex is available here: \textit{https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/LaTeX}} and Word form. This document, and other resources for the thesis, are provided in the resources folder of the corresponding Ilias group.

\section{References and Literature}

Good sources of literature for preparing a Computer Science thesis can be found here:

\begin{itemize}
\item IEEE Computer Society Digital Library: \url{http://www.computer.org/portal/site/csdl/index.jsp}
\item ACM Digital Library: \url{http://portal.acm.org/dl.cfm}
\item CEUR Workshop Proceedings: \url{http://ceur-ws.org/}
\item Springer Link: \url{http://www.springerlink.com/home/main.mpx}
\end{itemize}

The ACM and Springer documents are free of charge if accessed with a university IP (e.g. over a VPN connection or from the PI pool). For the IEEE library, the login details can be obtained at the university library. CEUR documents are all freely accessible. The bibliography has to be structured according to the specifications defined by the template, and citations should have this form: \cite{tannenbaum}, which is the IEEE referencing \cite{IEEEStyle}. This is taken care of automatically by the LaTeX template.

\section{Submission}

The two physical copies of the thesis, printed double-sided, must be submitted by the agreed deadline to the group’s office. In addition, a PDF version of the thesis along with all work products (e.g. code, models, questionnaires…) must be uploaded to the respective Ilias group. If the theses are not submitted by the agreed deadline, the examination conditions have not been satisfied and you will receive a grade 5.0.

Once the physical and soft copies of the thesis have been received, and all items borrowed from the group (e.g. keys, books) have been returned, the Studienbüro will be informed that the thesis has been successfully submitted. However, the thesis will NOT be graded, or certified as being sufficient for a passing grade, until after the submission deadline.
